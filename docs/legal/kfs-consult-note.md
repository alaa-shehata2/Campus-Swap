## Proposed working definition (for their review)

1. **Haram content** (prohibited class): alcohol/drugs facilitation, gambling
   (including betting tips/schemes), sexual services, and other content
   violating public morals as enumerated in the Terms.
2. **Medical / legal**: any diagnosis, treatment, dosage, or prescription
   advice; any legal advice or representation — both prohibited as listings
   and as "services" offered via exchange.
3. **Academic boundary**: tutoring, collaboration, code review, essay
   feedback, and project help allowed; submitting assessed work under
   another's name, exam impersonation, and contract cheating prohibited.

## Questions for student affairs

2. Is the academic-help vs. contract-cheating line drawn in the right place
   for KFS faculties?
3. Any wording they require verbatim in the Terms before launch?
4. A contact point for escalation of borderline cases during the pilot?
