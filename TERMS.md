# CampusSwap — Terms of Use (v1, DRAFT)

> **Status: DRAFT — not signed off.** Human-developer sign-off required before
> pilot launch (launch-gate item §10.1). Bracketed items need real details.
> Pilot scope: KFS University, English only, all times Cairo time (Africa/Cairo).

## 1. What CampusSwap is

A money-free exchange for KFS students: members publish Offers ("I give")
and Requests ("I need") for skills and physical items (lend / give / swap),
propose reciprocal exchanges, meet, complete, and review each other.
No money, credits, tips, payment links, or paid off-platform services —
anywhere on the platform (BR-1). Violations are removable; repeat violations
lead to suspension.

## 2. Who may join (D1, BR-3, BR-9)

- Any valid email may register (no university-email restriction).
- You must provide a display name, a campus/university (default
  `KFS University`), confirm you are **18 or older**, and accept the rules.
- Campus is **self-declared, not verified**: there is no "verified student"
  badge in the pilot. One account per person.
- Misrepresenting campus or age, and discovered under-age use, lead to
  suspension. 18+ only, no exceptions.

## 3. Listings (FR-L, BR-5)

- Offers and Requests, typed skill or item. Titles ≤80 chars, descriptions
  ≤2000 chars. Only Active listings are discoverable; max 20 active per member.
- Item modalities: `lend` (return expected — you must state the return
  date/duration), `give` (permanent), `swap` (describe the counterpart item).
- **Prohibited classes** (unpublishable; reported content fast-removed):
  (a) haram content — alcohol/drugs facilitation, gambling, sexual services,
  and other content violating public morals as enumerated here;
  (b) medical advice, services, diagnosis, or treatment;
  (c) legal advice or representation; (d) weapons; (e) stolen goods;
  (f) sexual services; (g) commercial advertising.
  Wording (a)–(c) is pending the KFS student-affairs consult (see
  `docs/legal/kfs-consult-note.md`); this draft is enforceable as written,
  and moderators enforce this enumerated list, not personal judgment.

## 4. Academic boundary (BR-6)

Allowed: tutoring, collaboration, code review, essay feedback, project help.
Prohibited: submitting assessed work under another's name, exam
impersonation, contract cheating. Borderline cases go to the moderation
lead; final wording follows the KFS consult.

## 5. Exchanges (FR-E, BR-2, BR-4, D7, D11)

- Every proposal links at least one listing per side plus written terms;
  one-sided proposals are rejected. Max 5 open proposals per listing; the
  listing auto-pauses on first acceptance. Terms freeze on acceptance.
- Scheduling needs a Cairo-labeled date/time and a meeting place. Public
  on-campus spots are recommended; private residences are allowed only by
  mutual agreement after an explicit safety reminder.
- Completion is two-step (Done → Confirm/Dispute within 7 days, else
  auto-complete). Cancellation needs a reason. Meet in person, on or near
  campus, by default.

## 6. Item loans (BR-7, D4)

The lender sets the return term per deal. **The platform has zero liability
for damage, loss, or theft**; borrowers owe return in the stated condition;
remedy is moderation plus reviews only. Disclaimers appear on lend creation,
proposal acceptance, and schedule confirmation — and on signup and listing
creation (5 flows, NFR-L-1).

## 7. Safety (D10, D11)

- 18+ only. Prefer public on-campus meeting spots; tell a friend where you
  are going. Private-residence meetings require mutual agreement.
- Stolen-item reports hide the listing first; law-enforcement handover
  happens only with platform-owner approval and preserved evidence.
- Dangerous content is triaged within 24h (target; pilot SLA median ≤48h).
  Report via any listing/user page or `[security contact — to be filled]`.

## 8. Reviews (FR-R, D6)

Blind and bilateral: one 1–5 score + optional text (≤1000 chars) per
participant, hidden until both submit or 14 days; 48h edit window; one
≤1000-char response by the reviewed party. Aggregate shows average + count
+ distribution + full history. No reviews on cancelled/disputed exchanges,
no self-reviews. Abusive reviews may be voided by moderation (voids logged).

## 9. Reports & moderation (FR-M)

- Any member may report any listing or user with a reason code, description
  (≥20 chars for "other"), and up to 3 images. Status updates
  (`Received → Under review → Resolved`) arrive in-app; the reported party
  is notified only if action is taken.
- Sanctions: hide listing, warn / suspend / ban user — every action logged
  with actor, reason, and timestamp. You may block or mute any user (stops
  their proposals/messages to you).

## 10. Accounts & content (P-3)

- Your email is used for login only: never displayed, sold, or shared.
- Deactivation hides your profile and listings immediately. Deletion /
  anonymization on request within 30 days, except legal/moderation holds
  disclosed in the privacy notice.
- Exchange messages and proposal history are anonymized 12 months after
  exchange closure; reports and moderation logs after 24 months.
- Moderators see message contents only on an opened case; every access is
  logged. Uploaded images must not expose location metadata to other users.

## 11. Liability & changes

The platform provides the venue only and assumes no liability for damage,
loss, theft, personal safety outcomes, or the quality/fairness of exchanges.
Durations are shown side-by-side before acceptance; fairness disputes are
resolved through reviews, not compensation. These Terms may change with
in-app notice; continued use means acceptance. Pilot-only service, provided
as-is.
